# About me

I'm a teacher at a university and a father. I pray best early in the morning, before the house is awake.
This year I'm making the Spiritual Exercises in daily life through a parish program, week by week.
I'm drawn to the Psalms and to the prodigal son. I often feel restless and pulled in many directions by work.
