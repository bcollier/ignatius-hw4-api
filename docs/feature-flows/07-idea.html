A retreat from an idea or a photo

“Trusting God with a new job”, or a photo of a page of verses, becomes a retreat of as many days as you choose.

The screen · iPhone
New retreat → or start from an idea or a photo

Server · FastAPI on Render
  1. POST /api/retreats with idea, idea_days and an optional photo: 202 at once.
  2. app/inspiration.py gives the model the idea and the photo; it answers with a title and, for each day, a Bible reference, or says the text is on the photo.
  3. The scripture is fetched from bible-api.com in the World English Bible (public domain), or copied from the photo. The model never writes scripture.
  4. The result is an ordinary source document: planned (feature 2) and built (feature 3) like any upload.
