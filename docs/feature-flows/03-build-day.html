Server · FastAPI on Render
  1. POST /api/retreats/{id}/days/{day}/build (or straight after planning). A background job; two run at a time.
  2. The reading is recorded straight away: it's the passage, word for word.
  3. For the heart: a reflection in a companion's voice, or the voice of Jesus, knowing the retreat so far and the days ahead.
  4. Deep dive: the model writes search queries; app/search.py runs every research service at once and merges the results. Claude also searches the web itself, and lists its sources.
  5. Guidance: the spoken lines (asking for the grace, before each reading, the silence, the closing) tailored to the day; the grace is kept word for word.
  6. Each part is recorded in its chosen voice, in parallel, with word timings for the follow-along text.
Data · Supabase, private (server key only)
  • retreats.datadays.{n}.tracks (reading, heart, deep: script, status, sources) and days.{n}.guide
  • bucket retreatsday{n}_{part}.mp3, .words.json timings, day{n}_research.json
  • llm_callsEach writing call with tokens, web searches and cost
