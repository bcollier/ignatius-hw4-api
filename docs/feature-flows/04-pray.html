Server · FastAPI on Render
  1. GET /api/retreats/{id} returns the retreat with a signed link, good for 24 hours, to each painting and recording in the private bucket.
  2. The browser plays the lectio sequence: ask for the grace · first reading · for the heart · second reading · deep dive · third reading · silence between two bells · last reading · closing.
  3. POST …/days/{day}/progress remembers where you stopped, so you can resume on another device; POST …/days/{day}/prayed marks the day prayed.
  4. GET /api/retreats/{id}/script.pdf prints the day or the whole retreat.
